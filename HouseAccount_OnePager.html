The opportunity

Home repair is a trust problem, not a search problem. HouseAccount vets and insures the pros, mediates the conversation over text, and stands behind the work — matching a homeowner to the right pro within a two-hour quote window. Because the platform reads every homeowner ↔ provider chat, that human triage layer is where speed, consistency, and cost live — exactly what the message-intelligence engine relieves.

HomeownersFree. Right pro, fair price, work covered ($2,000).
Service providersVetted + insured. Pay commission for ready-to-book work.
PartnersReal-estate channels routing repair & maintenance demand.
Part 1 — Marketplace (the business)
Request → match to vetted/insured pros (eligibility then rating) → quote in a 2-hour window → accept → book → pay after completion.
Part 2 — Message Intelligence (the leverage)
Every chat is classified, key facts extracted, and a deterministic rule routes it: automate the routine, escalate the risky, surface the whole picture to ops.

Design principles

  • Measured, not asserted — quality gated by an offline eval vs. a golden set.
  • Human-in-the-loop — money/trust/safety never auto-execute.
  • Deterministic orchestration — the model classifies; code routes.
  • Domain-separated data — marketplace, comms, analytics kept distinct.

MVP scope & what we watch

  • Request → match → quote → booking; ratings & reviews.
  • Classify · extract · route; aggregate insights console.
  • Watch: local liquidity, time-to-first-quote, complaint & human-review rates.
  • Deferred: nationwide launch, autonomous refunds, predictive pricing.
