HouseAccount — Platform Thinking

A trust-first home-services marketplace, with a message-intelligence engine that turns unstructured customer chats into routed, actionable insights.

Homeowners · Vetted Pros · Partners Marketplace + Message Intelligence Eval-first · Human-in-the-loop

01 — The OpportunityHome repair is a trust problem, not a search problem

Homeowners don't struggle to find a handyman — they struggle to find one they can trust to show up, do good work, and charge fairly. HouseAccount solves that by mediating the relationship: it vets and insures the pros, manages the initial conversation, and stands behind the work (lowest-price guarantee, $2,000 damage coverage). The homeowner describes the job; HouseAccount finds the right pro within a two-hour quote window; the homeowner reviews the quote and books.

The hidden bottleneck: because the platform manages all initial communication over text, every request, reschedule, price question, and complaint arrives as free-form chat that a human has to read, interpret, and route. That human triage layer is where speed, consistency, and cost live — and it's exactly what a message-intelligence engine is built to relieve.

03 — The core ideaTwo parts: the marketplace is the business, intelligence is the leverage

Part 1 — Marketplace

Trust-first matching

Request → match to vetted/insured pros (eligibility first, then rating) → quote in a 2-hour window → accept → book → pay after completion.

Part 2 — Message Intelligence

Chats into actions

Every customer chat is classified, key facts are extracted, and a deterministic rule routes it: automate the routine, escalate the risky, and surface the whole picture to ops.

04 — How the engine thinksThe model judges; explicit code decides

Keeping orchestration out of the model makes every decision auditable and safe to automate.

Customer chat→ Classify intent→ Extract entities + sentiment→ Deterministic route→ Auto-act · Escalate · Aggregate

Six intents: new request · reschedule · cancellation · complaint · pricing question · follow-up. Low-risk new requests auto-create a job; complaints, cancellations, negative sentiment, high urgency, or low confidence are held for a human with a stated reason.

05 — Design principlesWhat keeps it trustworthy at scale

Measured, not asserted

Quality is gated by an offline eval against a hand-verified golden set — a number, not a claim.

Human-in-the-loop

Money, trust, and safety decisions never auto-execute; they route to a person.

Domain-separated data

Marketplace, communications, and analytics stay distinct for governance and scale.

Deterministic orchestration

The model classifies; code routes — so behavior is explainable and testable.
